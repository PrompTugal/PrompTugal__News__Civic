## The focus, in Dinis's words (17 November 2026)

"Public data, public services and the typical bureaucracy that is important, that very few understand, and that has very few cross-mappings."

## As briefed (17 November 2026)

Three parts:

1. **Public money**: public procurement (base.gov.pt), council decisions, agendas and minutes, the Diário da República.
2. **How public services work, step by step, from official sources** (gov.pt, ePortugal, the councils): paying a bill by Multibanco reference, getting a NIF, the SNS, AIMA residency, which office does what.
3. **The cross-mappings**: council, contract, company, place.

Start with the Algarve's 16 councils. Independent and labelled so: never present the site as official or imitate a government body's look. Public facts only, each with its source and date read; people only as public and professional; check each portal's terms and robots before reading it, and record any refusal as a gap.

The first version (from the five-sites brief): the repo to the standard; the site live at its domain; a front page and three to five core pages with real data; one design pass of three front-page variations at `/design/`; `REALITY.md` true; a hand-over debrief; five lines to the conductor; then stop.
